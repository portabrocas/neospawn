# Fan favorites checklist

What players loved (and disliked) about OreSpawn and Crazy Craft, gathered from mod pages,
wikis, forums and the other OreSpawn remakes. Go through it and mark each line
**Must / Nice / Skip** in the last column; I'll build the musts into the roadmap.

Status: ✅ in NeoSpawn · 🟡 partly · ❌ missing · 🔜 already planned

## Where this came from, and its limits

- The OreSpawn wikis (feature pages, the fan **Suggestions** page, the full page index)
- Mod listing pages that describe the original's headline features (9Minecraft, MinecraftXL)
- A Feed the Beast forum thread, *"Your Thoughts on Orespawn?"*: mostly criticism, useful for what to avoid
- The Crazy Craft wiki's full mod list
- The pages of other OreSpawn remakes (OreSpawn Community Edition, Craziness Awakened, Chaos
  Persists), which show what players keep asking those projects for
- **Not covered:** Reddit blocks automated access, and YouTube comments weren't readable. Those are
  where the most nostalgia lives. If you remember favorites from there, add them at the bottom.

## What players complained about (avoid these)

| Complaint | Where | NeoSpawn |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildly overpowered**: ~1000-damage swords one-shot every boss in every other mod; "you run out of things to do fast" | FTB forum | Partly addressed: weapons top out at 80 damage, bosses need real gear. Keep an eye on it |
| An ant at spawn teleports you to a dimension full of diamonds, which skips all progression | FTB forum | **Decided:** realm charms gate every ant dimension (docs/ROSTER.md) |
| Breaks modpacks: plays badly with other mods | FTB forum | Keep vanilla-style tags and recipes, and test alongside the pack |
| Bosses with thousands of health feel tedious (fans asked for The King to go from 7000 to 3000) | wiki Suggestions | Health caps at 1024; the polish pass tunes how long fights take |
| Performance: per-tick costs from huge numbers of mobs | remake notes | Keep spawn weights sane; profile once dimensions exist |

## Good to know: other remakes exist

People are already trying to bring OreSpawn to modern versions, and NeoSpawn should stand out
from them:

- **An unofficial NeoForge 1.21.1 port of the original's code** (on GitHub). It aims to be an exact
  copy, bugs included. Because it's built from the original code, it carries the copyright risk we're
  avoiding. NeoSpawn's original code and art are its long-term advantage.
- **Craziness Awakened**, a remake for 1.20.1. It skips girlfriends.
- **OreSpawn Community Edition**, which reuses the original's textures and models and has 100+ romanceable characters.
- **Chaos Persists** and **Bifröst**, other remakes.

What sets NeoSpawn apart: original and publishable, modern design, tested, and balanced so the absurd
fun lasts longer.

## Decisions (2026-10-14)

- **Build order:** rideable creatures, then zoo cages, then the other Musts.
- **Modpack:** include everything from Crazy Craft that has a modern version; close substitutes are fine
  where the original has no 1.21.1 version.
