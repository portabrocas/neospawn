## Install
Put `neospawn-0.21.4.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## Changes in 0.21.4
- **Fewer Kings in Utopia.** Trees of Goodness (each guarded by The King) used to generate in about 1 in 90 chunks
  with nothing stopping them from clustering, so a short walk could turn up several Kings. Now there is at most one
  per 384×384-block area, and two are never closer than 160 blocks: about six times fewer Kings.
- **Empress Trees can't cluster either.** Same average rarity as before, but always at least 128 blocks apart.
- Only newly generated Utopia land is affected. Kings already in a world stay; remove extras with
  `/kill @e[type=neospawn:the_king]` while standing near them.
