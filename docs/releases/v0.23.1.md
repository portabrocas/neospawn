## Install
Put `neospawn-0.23.1.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## New in 0.23.1: hand-painted mob skins
- **59 mobs get hand-painted skins** in place of the generated ones: every quadruped, the theropods and humanoids,
  and hero mobs including the ostrich, peacock (eyespot tail), realm ants (banded in their colors), Giant Mantis,
  Sovereign Scorpion, Hercules Beetle, Leviathan (glowing side spots), whale, Basilisk (diamondback) and the
  Moth Titan. Faces, mouths, hooves, claws, stripes and spots are all drawn on.
- Small eyes (frogs, lizards and others) always show a dark pupil, so they no longer vanish into the skin.

Everything from 0.23.0 (Utopia revamp, GeckoLib Empress, Gorgantua and Kraken, Arcane Tanzanite, new item art)
is included.
