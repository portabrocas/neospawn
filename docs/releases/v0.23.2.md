## Install
Put `neospawn-0.23.2.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## Fixed in 0.23.2: Utopia is playable again
Arriving in Utopia could freeze the game for many seconds: several Kings woke at once and their attacks set the
golden meadows and forests on fire.
- **Bosses sleep until you come near.** A boss with no player within 96 blocks naps (no AI, no boss bar) and wakes
  when you come within 48 blocks or hit it. Around one player only the nearest two bosses fight; the rest wait
  their turn.
- **Boss attacks no longer start fires.** Beams, fireballs and meteors still explode; lightning still strikes.
- **No more falling gravel or lava in Utopia's floating islands.**
- **Boss trees are further apart:** a Tree of Goodness every 40 chunks (at least 16 apart), an Empress Tree every
  32 (at least 12), and Empress Trees at least 10 chunks from a King's.

Only newly generated Utopia land gets the new spacing. Kings already in a world fall asleep instead of all
attacking at once; to remove extras, stand near them and use `/kill @e[type=neospawn:the_king,distance=..200]`.
