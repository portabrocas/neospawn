## Install
Put `neospawn-0.23.0.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## New in 0.23.0: the revamp
- **Utopia, the golden sky paradise:** permanent golden hour over rolling golden meadows, with floating islands
  above. New blocks: Golden Grass, Sky Fluff (no fall damage), Sunbloom, Honeybell and Starpetal (they glow, and
  fit in pots) and the full Aurelian wood set with glowing leaves.
- **Utopia structures:** the King's and Empress's guardian trees (a hollow trunk with a spiral stair, a root cellar,
  and a crown platform), the Gilded Gazebo, the Honey Orchard, the Sunlit Library and its observatory, and the Sky
  Temple with a cloud stair down to the meadow. Small pests (Gilded Wasps, Bookmites, Cloud Wisps, termites) guard them.
- **Utopia creatures:** the rideable Gilded Stag and the glowing Sunwing Moth.
- **Arcane Tanzanite:** an Arcane armor set and self-enchanting Tanzanite tools.
- **GeckoLib bosses:** new models for the Empress (crystal butterfly queen), Gorgantua and the Kraken.
- **Art pass:** redrawn icons, distinct armor sets and boss weapons.
