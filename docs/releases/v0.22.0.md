## Install
Put `neospawn-0.22.0.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## New in 0.22.0: everyday mobs drop useful things
- **Nine new drops** from 33 regular mobs: Chitin Plate (bugs, ants, crabs), Venom Gland (scorpions, warbees,
  jellies), Ectoplasm (ghosts), Glow Sac (fireflies, sprites), Insect Wing (butterflies, moths, dragonflies), Dino
  Hide and the rarer Raptor Claw (dinosaurs), Stink Gland (stinkpups, stink beetles) and Kaboom Core (Kaboombas).
- **What they make:** Chitin armor (full set: Haste) and Dino Hide armor (full set: Speed); Antivenom; venom
  arrows; the Ghost Lantern (invisible creatures glow); Spectral Glass; the Firefly Jar (full light, can't start
  fires); glowing arrows; the Featherfall Cloak; Climbing Claws; Stink Bombs; and the Mining Charge (a walkable
  1x2 shaft, 12 deep).
- **Second recipes** for the Bug and Creeper Repellents, Butterfly Wings and the Tunnel Charge. The old recipes
  still work.
- **Ant hills** (Worker, Fire, Prism, Volatile): ants climb out until four are nearby. Build farms and
  enclosures; a redstone signal pauses them.
- A new Field Guide chapter covers all of it.

## Morph and accessory slots
- Morph: amphibious animals swim, the Sky Shark breathes water, ghosts are undead, venomous mobs resist their own
  venom, bombs and robots ignore explosions, bosses scare monsters away, and mounts can carry a rider.
- Redstone Sneakers work in a Curios feet slot or the Accessories shoes slot as well as the boots slot. Every
  NeoSpawn wearable now works in Accessories slots too (Accessories ships with The Aether).

## Spawns
- A mob won't spawn naturally where enough of its own kind already roam, so one species can't flood an area.
- Stinging and grabbing sea creatures no longer spawn on Peaceful; cave monsters respect Mushroom Fields.
