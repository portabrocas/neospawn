The first public build of NeoSpawn: an original monster-and-ore mega mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge 21.1)**,
inspired by OreSpawn and Crazy Craft. All code and art are new.

## Install
1. Install NeoForge 21.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Prism, CurseForge or the NeoForge installer).
2. Put **GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1** (4.9.x) in your `mods` folder. It is required.
3. Download `neospawn-0.21.3.jar` below into the same `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`.

Optional mods NeoSpawn works with: Morph, Curios, Better Combat, Lootr, EMI, Biomes O' Plenty, Terralith.

## What's in it
- 67 mobs and 27 bosses (The King, Gorgantua, the Kraken, The Empress, The Ascended King and more), with
  telegraphed attacks, phases and stuns, and rival bosses that brawl.
- Six ant realms, each opened by its own charm, and nine hand-built structures with tiered loot.
- Ruby, Tanzanite, Titanium, Uranium, Salt and Fossil ores; Paragon gear; boss-scale armor; the Behemoth Blade;
  gadgets, repellents, Zoo Cages, mounts and mechs; ore trees and the Duplicator Tree.
- The Royal Egg line, the Gorgantua egg quest, the Summoning Altar, the Easter Bunny and the Field Guide.
- Cristian, Redstone Sneakers and Butterfly Wings.
