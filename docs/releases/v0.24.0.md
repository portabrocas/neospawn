## Install
Put `neospawn-0.24.0.jar` in your `mods` folder, replacing any older `neospawn-*.jar`. GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1
(4.9.x) is still required.

## New in 0.24.0
- **The Gobbler**, a mini-boss: a giant chomping ball that hops out of plains and forests at night, eating logs,
  leaves, dirt and sand in its way (never stone, and only with mobGriefing on). It roars up ghosts that drift through
  walls. Eat its **Power Pellet** and the ghosts flee, and one hit pops them. It drops the **Gobbler's Jaw** and
  **Arcade Tokens**, plus one or two treasures from other mods: Inventory Pets, ancient ingots, runes, lucky blocks.
  The odds follow each item's rarity, so common finds are likely and epic ones are rare. Summon it at the altar
  with a cake.
- **The Rift Gun**, a rare find in strongholds, ancient cities, end cities and the toughest NeoSpawn vaults. It is
  never crafted. Right-click a wall, floor or ceiling for a blue rift, or sneak for an orange one. Walk, fall or
  throw things into one and out they come from the other, momentum and all. Each shot costs an ender pearl.
- **Spring Basket**: place one within 8 blocks of an Easter Bunny and it lays eggs three times as often. That makes
  a captured bunny a proper egg farm.
- **NeoSpawn advancements**: a tab of 41 milestones (ores, creatures, mob drops, gadgets, all six realms, every
  boss), rewarding experience.
- **Quest book for the NeoSpawnPack** (FTB Quests, needs FTB Library and FTB Teams): 12 chapters and 127 quests
  covering NeoSpawn and the pack's other mods. NeoSpawn quests complete through the advancements above. Rewards are
  capped per tier, so nothing skips progression. It is not part of the jar: install it with
  `tools/install-quests.ps1`.
